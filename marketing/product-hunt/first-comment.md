# Product Hunt — Maker First Comment

Hey Product Hunt — maker here 👋

BallotWatch came out of something that had been bothering me for a while: we live in a culture that's absolutely saturated with political *opinion*, but strangely starved of political *record*. Everyone has takes. Almost nobody — myself included, honestly — could tell you how their own representative voted last week, or what Congress actually passed this month.

And it's not because the information is secret. It's all public: every roll-call vote, every bill, every campaign contribution. It's just scattered across half a dozen government sites and written like it's designed not to be read. A single bill can run hundreds of pages of legal cross-references. So we outsource our understanding to headlines and hot takes, and the actual record goes unread.

BallotWatch is my attempt to close that gap:

- **Type your address** → meet your representatives (no account needed)
- **See every vote they cast** — real roll calls, on the record
- **Read any bill in plain English** — this is where AI earns its keep. It takes the hundreds of pages of legalese and tells you what the bill *actually does*, in a paragraph
- **Follow the money** — campaign contributions, from FEC data

One thing I cared a lot about: it's deliberately non-partisan by design. Party affiliation is a small text label, not a color-coded identity. No spin, no scoring, no "this bill is good/bad." Just the record, made readable.

I'd genuinely love to hear what you'd want from a tool like this — especially: what's the one thing about Congress you've always wished you could look up but never bothered to dig for?

Thanks for checking it out 🗳️
