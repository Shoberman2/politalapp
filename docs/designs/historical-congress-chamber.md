## Vision

### The product idea, as built
An interactive **Senate seating chart** showing all 100 desks with the actual senator who held that desk, scrubble across **27 Congresses (93rd-119th, 1973-2026)**. Click a desk → see who held it, their bio, their famous votes. Click "Webster's desk" → see the **lineage** of senators back to 1836. Color-code the chart at famous moments (Iraq War vote, ACA, impeachments) to see **how every senator voted spatially**. House gets an **honest composition view** — no fake assigned seats; instead a hemicycle showing party balance year over year.

Backed by a **full bill archive** of every bill introduced in Congress 1973-now (~250K-500K bills), every roll-call vote, every sponsor, every committee referral. The site becomes the **canonical 50-year archive of Congress** with civic-tech-grade depth and editorial-grade presentation.

### Why this is novel
Nobody — GovTrack, Congress.gov, Quorum, Open States, ProPublica — has built an interactive historical Senate seat chart. The Senate Historical Office publishes the data; nobody has glued it to member profiles + votes + bill outcomes in one editorial visualization. This is the **eureka**: the conventional approach (tables of names) misses the spatial story. The Senate floor IS the institution, and visualizing it across time makes a year-by-year power-map of America.

### 10x check (delivered, not aspirational)
The scope already adopted — 27 Congresses, lineage, flips, historic moments, full bill archive — IS the 10x version. There is no "10x bigger" that's still in BallotWatch's scope. The bigger move would be a separate product (longitudinal voting-pattern analysis as a B2B research tool), not a feature of this site.

## Accepted Scope (final agreed v1)

### Visualization
- **Senate seating chart** for every Congress from 93rd (1973) to 119th (current).
- **100 desks** rendered editorially per DESIGN.md — Instrument Serif labels, accent #1D4ED8, no party-block fills as card backgrounds.
- **Click desk → senator profile** (existing `PoliticianDetail.jsx`).
- **"Through this desk" lineage panel** — every senator who held the desk back to 1836 (where data exists).
- **"Who flipped this seat" overlay** — light up desks that changed party between Congresses, with hover detail.
- **Historic moments mode** — freeze chart at curated famous votes (10-15 initial: Iraq War '02, Bush tax cuts '01 + '03, ACA '10, Trump impeachments '20 + '21, Inflation Reduction Act '22, etc.); color-code each desk by senator's vote.
- **Year/Congress scrubber** — slide through Congresses; chart re-paints.
- **House composition view (honest)** — hemicycle of 435 dots colored by party. NO fake assigned seats. Explicit "House seating is open — these positions are illustrative" disclosure per `[editorial-no-ai-badges-per-sentence]` learning (editorial integrity > visual fiction).

### Data backfill
- **All members 1973-now** across both chambers: ~5,000-6,000 unique members. Bioguide IDs, photos (Congress.gov where available, placeholder otherwise), party, state/district, terms served.
- **All bills 1973-now**: HR, S, HJRES, SJRES, HCONRES, SCONRES, HRES, SRES — every bill type. Per-Congress, this is ~10K-20K bills. Total: ~270K-540K bills.
- **All roll-call votes 1973-now**: Senate.gov XML feeds + House clerk + Congress.gov. Per-member vote records.
- **Senate desk assignments per Congress**: hand-curated from Senate Historical Office data where available (likely full data 1989+, partial pre-1989).
- **Senate desk lineage**: full lineage table per famous desk (Webster, Clay, candy desk, etc.) — start with the ~15 famous desks Senate Historical Office tracks; backfill others where lineage is sourceable.

### Out of scope (explicit, with rationale)
- AI explanations for historical bills (deferred — see TODOS).
- Desk drawer inscriptions (sourcing risk — Phase 3).
- House individual seat assignments (does not exist; structural reality).
- Career-longitudinal voting-pattern analysis (separate feature).
- Cross-chamber moves (rep → senator) visualized in one chart (Phase 2 — additive later).
- Live "where senators are sitting RIGHT NOW today" (no such data feed; only per-Congress assignments).

## Deferred to TODOS.md (committed follow-ups)

- **AI explanations for top-traffic historical bills** — once 6-12 weeks of engagement data shows which historical bills get views, generate AI explanations for the top ~500 (cost-bounded). Reuses existing `bill_explanations` server-side cache.
- **Senate desk drawer inscriptions** — photo + transcription sourcing, permissions check, editorial review. Phase 3 prestige feature.
- **Career arcs on PoliticianDetail** — for any member, plot every desk they sat in (or for House: every term + party block) across their career. Pure UI feature on top of historical data.
- **Extend lineage beyond famous desks** — backfill lineage for all 100 desks (not just the ~15 Senate-Historical-Office-tracked famous ones). Data is sourceable but more curation work.

## Major Open Risks Surfaced in Step 0 (to be addressed in sections below)

1. **Senate desk fidelity pre-1989** — Senate Historical Office digital data thins for older Congresses. Fallback: per-Congress confidence flag; show "composition-only" view for low-fidelity Congresses with clear disclosure.
2. **House structural reality** — must NOT pretend House has assigned seats. Editorial trust depends on this.
3. **ETL backfill duration + Congress.gov rate limits** — 27 Congresses × tens of thousands of bills + votes is multi-day-to-week wall-clock backfill. Needs chunking, resumability, sentinel state (`[long-backfill-sentinel]` learning).
4. **Storage costs** — 270K-540K bills + votes + member-Congress terms could exceed Supabase tier. Needs capacity check before backfill.
5. **Photo coverage for ~6,000 historical members** — already hard for current members (recent commit `e0b5592`). Will be worse historically. Need graceful placeholder + best-effort enrichment.
6. **Schema migration of every hardcoded `congress = 119` reference** — 10+ files. Single-Congress assumptions are baked in.
7. **RLS for every new table** — `senate_desks`, `senate_desk_assignments`, `senate_desk_lineage`, `member_congress_terms`, etc. Each must have explicit policy in migration (`[supabase-rls-default-deny]`).
8. **Historic-moments curation** — picking which 10-15 votes are "famous" is editorial responsibility. Methodology must be visible to user.

**To address in Section 1 (Architecture):**
- Data-source URLs/formats/coverage years missing. Plan cites "Senate Historical Office" 4x with no machine-readable format. Pre-1993 Congress.gov coverage is incomplete (text, summaries, sponsors thin). Need explicit Data Sources table with format/coverage/license.

**To address in Section 4 (Edge Cases):**
- Mid-Congress senator changes: deaths, resignations, appointments, special elections (one desk can have 2-3 senators in a single Congress). Schema must time-range assignments, not single-row.
- Party switchers break "flipped seat" logic: Jeffords '01, Specter '09, Sinema '22.
- Contested elections (Franken/Coleman 2008-2009, MN seat empty 8 months).
- Redistricting changes House `state/district` between decades — historical members table needs term-scoped district, not member-scoped.
- House → Senate transitions need bioguide deduping but unified member view.

**To address in Section 7 (Performance):**
- Vote rows are the row-count bomb, not bills: 100 senators × thousands of votes × 27 Congresses ≈ 5-10M rows. Plus House: 435 × thousands × 27 = much more. Realistic total: 50-100M vote-rows.
- Supabase free tier (500MB) blown immediately. Pro tier (8GB) pressured. Need capacity sizing pre-backfill.
- Congress.gov rate limit (5000 req/hr) × 540K bills + votes ≈ weeks of wall-clock backfill.

**To address in Section 9 (Deployment / Sequencing):**
- Plan is 3-4 features stapled together: (a) multi-Congress schema migration, (b) member backfill, (c) bill+vote backfill, (d) seat chart + lineage + moments, (e) House hemicycle. Each is its own PR sequence. Land in order, validate each.
- 1-Congress pre-flight probe before full backfill (e.g., 95th Congress 1977) to validate data sources.

**To address in Section 10 (Long-term):**
- "v1" honest sequencing: Land schema + 1-Congress probe before backfilling 27. Treat the seat chart + moments as Phase 2/3 inside the same overall plan, not as v1 sub-features.

**Consistency fixes (applied inline below):**

## Decisions made during live review (Sections 1-11)

### 1.A Schema for time-ranged member-Congress facts → `member_congress_terms` join table
New table: `(bioguide_id, congress, chamber, state, district, party, caucus, term_start, term_end, reason_for_end)`. One row per member-term. Handles mid-Congress changes (resignations, party switches, contested seats). Existing `members` rows get a one-time migration into initial terms.

### 1.B Data source strategy → Multi-source with reconciliation + fidelity tier
- Primary 1993+: Congress.gov.
- Bills 1973-1992: GovInfo fallback.
- Senate roll calls 1989+: Senate.gov XML.
- House roll calls: House Clerk + Congress.gov reconciled.
- Desk assignments + lineage: Senate Historical Office (machine-readable 1989+; hand-curated pre-1989).
- Per-Congress `fidelity_tier` flag in `congress_metadata`: `full` / `partial` / `composition-only`. Surfaced in UI as a disclosure banner per Congress.

### 4.A Historic moments curation → Manually curated + visible methodology page
Hand-pick 10-15 famous Senate votes (cross-decade balance: Bush, Obama, Trump, Biden). Criteria: nationally-discussed within 90 days, AP/NYT front-page coverage, lasting policy impact. Public methodology page lists criteria + every included moment + why. Illustrative initial list (to be finalized in Phase 5): Bush tax cuts '01 + '03, Iraq War '02, ACA '10, Sequester '13, ACA repeal failure '17, TCJA '17, Trump impeachments '20 + '21, ARP '21, IRA '22, CHIPS '22, Bipartisan Infra '21, Respect for Marriage '22, Israel/Ukraine supplemental '24.

### 4.B Party color treatment → Tinted desk backgrounds + saturated party border
- Desk fill: ~10% opacity tint of party color over `#FFFFFF`.
- Desk border: 1px saturated party color.
- D: `#2563EB` (fill `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.10)`).
- R: `#DC2626` (fill `rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.10)`).
- I: `#7C3AED` (fill `rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.10)`).
- Vacancies: neutral `#9C9789` border, no fill.
- Mid-Congress party switcher: split tint driven by per-term ranges.
- **DESIGN.md update is a v1 (Phase 5) deliverable** — add the "tinted party fills" pattern as a documented chamber-specific exception to the "party as metadata, not identity" principle.

### 7.A Preflight strategy → 1-Congress probe first (95th Congress, 1977-78)
Phase 0 of the rollout. Validates Congress.gov coverage, Senate.gov URL pattern, identity reconciliation, storage footprint, render performance against ONE Congress before committing to the full 27-Congress backfill.

### 7.B Capacity strategy → Stay on Supabase Pro, accept ~$1-3/mo overage
Estimated 6-8 GB total. Pro tier includes 8 GB. Overage at ~$0.125/GB-month. No cold-archive infra in v1.

### 9.A Phasing strategy → 5-phase sequenced PRs (see Phase rollout sequence below)

### 11.A Design review → /plan-design-review next
Significant new UI surface. Run `/plan-design-review` after this CEO review completes. Biggest open design question: mobile chamber rendering (Senate hemicycle on <768px). Lock in tinted-fills pattern formally.

### Photo coverage → Defer to TODOS.md (P3)
Silhouette placeholders for missing historical members in v1. Future enhancement to source photos from Wikipedia / Library of Congress Bioguide if engagement signal supports it.

## Design Review Decisions (2026-05-21)

Supersedes high-level UI specs in Section 11 of the CEO review. Mockup generation was unavailable (OpenAI org verification still pending — pre-existing TODO). Text-only review with detailed ASCII wireframes + mechanical token derivation from DESIGN.md + locked CEO-review constraints.

### D2 — Desk visual content → number + senator last name
- Each desk shows desk number (Geist Mono 9px top) + senator last name (General Sans 9.5px medium below).
- Famous desks (Webster's, Clay's, candy desk, etc.) get a 3px solid #1D4ED8 accent dot in the top-right corner of the desk.
- Mobile fallback: desk number only (name revealed on tap-to-reveal).
- Editorial broadsheet principle: density IS the moment; the chart reads at-a-glance without interaction.

### D3 — Lineage panel position → slide-in drawer from the right (desktop)
- Desktop (≥1024px): drawer slides in from right covering 40% of viewport (448px).
- Tablet (768-1023px): centered modal.
- Mobile (<768px): centered modal (same as tablet).
- Chart stays visible behind drawer on desktop (drawer backdrop is partial — rgba(26,26,24,0.20) over the chamber side, NOT full-screen).
- 200ms ease-out slide; Esc closes; focus returns to invoking desk.

### D4 — Scrubber learning affordance → inline editorial hint that auto-dismisses
- Caption below scrubber: "Drag the marker to change Congress, or click any year." in General Sans Italic 12px #6B6861.
- Fades out 400ms after the user successfully scrubs once. localStorage key `chamber_scrubber_taught_v1`.
- Honors Krug "instructions must die" by being self-removing rather than permanent UI furniture.

### D5 — Mobile chamber rendering → compact hemicycle with tap-to-reveal
- Hemicycle scaled to fit 375px viewport.
- Each desk shrinks to ~20×28px, showing only desk number (Geist Mono 8px).
- Tap any desk → small inline label appears above the chart with senator name + party (3-sec timeout, or until another desk tapped).
- Second tap on same desk → full lineage drawer (modal on mobile).
- Hit areas expand invisibly to ≥44×44px for touch.
- Editorial integrity: the spatial story (hemicycle) survives at mobile — never falls back to a flat list.

### D6 — Historic moments overlay → vote-outcome tint REPLACES party tint
- When overlay is active, party tint disappears entirely; desk fills with vote-outcome color:
  - Yea: rgba(22,163,74,0.10) over white + 1px #16A34A border.
  - Nay: rgba(220,38,38,0.10) over white + 1px #DC2626 border.
  - Not voting / Absent: 1px dashed #9C9789, no fill.
- Legend below chart explains the swap.
- Vote tally rendered as text adjacent to moment heading: e.g. "ACA, 12/24/2009 · 60-39 · Senate Yea" in Geist Mono 14px. NOT a gauge per [editorial-popover-anti-gauge] learning.
- Spatial layout itself preserves party context (Dems on left, Reps on right) so the swap doesn't lose party signal entirely.

### A1 — Time-ranged composite primary keys

- `member_congress_terms`: PK `(bioguide_id, congress, term_start)`
- `senate_desk_assignments`: PK `(congress, desk_id, assigned_at)`

Handles mid-Congress party switchers (Specter '09, Sinema '22, Jeffords '01), resignations + appointments (Kennedy → Brown 2010), contested-election vacancies (Franken/Coleman 8-month MN gap). Each distinct stint of being-in-a-state is a distinct row.

### A2 — Voteview.com as primary source for pre-1993 identity mapping

UCSD Voteview's hand-maintained ICPSR ↔ bioguide crosswalk (used by every political-science researcher) ingests first in P1. Available as CSV dump from voteview.com. Adds one external data dependency but covers ~6,000 members 1789-now with high-confidence alias resolution.

Implementation note: ingest the full Voteview crosswalk into `member_id_aliases(canonical_bioguide, alias_source, alias_id)` with `alias_source = 'voteview_icpsr'` for all rows. Future-extensible to other ID schemes.

### A3 — Local-laptop backfill with resumable state

`etl/backfillHistorical.ts` runs as a long-running CLI process from a dev machine (not GitHub Actions). State persists in `backfill_state` table. Process can be paused / restarted at any point. Matches `[long-backfill-sentinel]` learning.

GitHub Actions cap at 6 hours wall-clock; the 14-day backfill doesn't fit. Local execution with resumable state is the path that works without new infra.

**Sentinel coordination:** daily ETL detects `backfill_state.status = 'running'` and skips itself for the day. Resumes normal operation when backfill completes.

**Error budget:** 3 consecutive Congress 5xx errors halt backfill (alert). Per-record malformed JSON → skip + log to `backfill_errors`, do not halt. Per-source 404 (e.g. Senate.gov URL pattern shift) → mark Congress as `fidelity=partial` and continue.

### Q2 — Hybrid SOT between `members` and `member_congress_terms`

`members` table keeps current-snapshot fields (party, state, district denormalized). `member_congress_terms` is the full historical record AND the current term. A Postgres trigger on `member_congress_terms` INSERT/UPDATE syncs `members.party/state/district` to the most-recent term.

**Existing single-Congress queries continue to work unchanged.** Historical queries route through terms. Migration to historical depth is non-breaking.

**REGRESSION TEST (CRITICAL):** verify `members` never drifts from `member_congress_terms.most_recent` after 1000 simulated term inserts in random order. Per Iron Rule from /plan-eng-review.

### P1 — Vercel edge cache for `/chamber/:congress`

- Past Congresses (≤118th): `s-maxage=86400, stale-while-revalidate=604800` (24h fresh, 7d stale).
- Current Congress (119th): `s-maxage=3600, stale-while-revalidate=86400` (1h fresh, 24h stale).
- Daily ETL completion for 119th Congress triggers a cache invalidation via Vercel API.

Sub-100ms TTFB globally. ~99% cache hit for past Congresses. Pattern proven in this codebase for `bill_explanations`.

## RLS Policy Specification (P0 migration)

ALTER TABLE <table> ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY;
CREATE POLICY "anon_read" ON <table> FOR SELECT TO anon USING (true);
